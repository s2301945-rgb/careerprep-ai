# CareerPrep AI

## Project Overview

CareerPrep AI is an AI-powered career preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for their future careers. It provides tools to improve job application skills, practice interviews, and identify career opportunities.

## Main Features

### 1. AI Resume Builder
Helps users create and improve their resumes by providing suggestions for formatting, content, and relevant skills.

### 2. AI Mock Interview
Allows users to practice answering interview questions and receive AI-generated feedback to improve their confidence and communication skills.

### 3. Career and Job Matching
Helps users explore career opportunities based on their skills, interests, qualifications, and career goals.
